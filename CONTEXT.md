# Problem Pulse

A private research engine that discovers recurring user problems from public online discussions.

## Language

### Pipeline entities

**RawDocument**:
Untransformed content fetched from a collector, before normalization.
_Avoid_: Post, entry, item, submission

**Document**:
Normalized, source-agnostic representation of collected content. Immutable evidence.
_Avoid_: Post, entry, normalized post

**ClassifiedDocument**:
Transient processing result — the output of AI classification for a Document, containing extracted Pains, Evidence, BuyingSignals, Persona, Sentiment and other classification metadata. Not a long-lived business entity.
_Avoid_: AnalyzedDocument, EnrichedDocument

**Evidence**:
The smallest unit of source material that supports the existence of a Pain. An immutable excerpt from a Document with character offset and confidence score.
_Avoid_: Snippet, quote, citation

**Pain**:
A semantic interpretation of a problem, derived from one or more Evidence fragments. The atomic unit of analysis — what gets embedded, clustered, and scored.
_Avoid_: PainPoint, problem, complaint, issue

**Cluster**:
Canonical grouping of semantically equivalent or highly similar Pains. Answers: "which Pains are the same thing?"
_Avoid_: Group, category, topic

**Opportunity**:
A Cluster enriched with market analysis and prioritisation signals to represent a potential product or business opportunity. The terminal domain concept.
_Avoid_: Insight, finding, lead

### Value objects

**Embedding**:
Immutable vector representation of a Pain, generated by an embedding model. Derived, replaceable, and carries model metadata (version, dimensions, content hash for staleness detection).
_Avoid_: Vector, encoding

### Source and metadata

**Source**:
The logical origin platform of a Document (e.g. `reddit`, `github`, `hacker_news`). Independent of the collection mechanism or platform-specific metadata.
_Avoid_: Platform, origin, provider

### Classifications (Document-level)

**Sentiment**:
The emotional polarity and intensity expressed by the author toward the subject. Purely linguistic — describes how the author writes, not how costly the problem is.
Values: `very_negative`, `negative`, `neutral`, `positive`, `very_positive`
_Avoid_: Tone, mood, emotion

**BuyingSignal**:
Evidence-backed indication of demand for a solution — including willingness to pay, active tool search, replacement intent, manual workarounds, or internal tools built as substitutes.
_Avoid_: Intent, demand signal, purchase signal

**Persona**:
Who is speaking — the professional or demographic role of the author.
_Avoid_: Role, author type, user type

### Classifications (Pain-level)

**Severity**:
The estimated impact of a Pain on the affected user's outcomes, considering time, money, risk, frequency, and operational disruption. Distinct from Sentiment (emotional tone) and BuyingSignal (demand).
Values: `trivial`, `low`, `medium`, `high`, `critical`
_Avoid_: Priority, importance, urgency

**TargetMarket**:
The market segment primarily affected by the Pain (e.g. finance teams, independent developers, small businesses). Distinct from Persona (who is speaking vs. who has the problem).
_Avoid_: Audience, user segment, customer type

### Non-domain concepts

**Projection**:
A rendered view over one or more domain entities for a specific audience or purpose. Reports, dashboards, API responses, and exports are all projections — they present knowledge but own none of it. Not a domain entity.
_Avoid_: Report, view, output

## Invariants

- A Pain must have a current embedding before it can participate in clustering.
- Every Pain must reference at least one Evidence fragment.
- Evidence is immutable and always points to a single Document.
- Sentiment, Persona, and BuyingSignal belong to the Document (how the author communicated).
- Severity and TargetMarket belong to the Pain (what problem exists and who it affects).
- A Cluster can exist without qualifying as an Opportunity.
